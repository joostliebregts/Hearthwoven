# Changelog

Every tweak that reaches a PC or the server gets its own patch version (Joost, 2026-10-08), so the version in the
log line and in Gale always says which build runs.

## 0.8.1 (2026-10-10, client and server release)
This is the first release since 0.6.5. It brings everything from 0.7, 0.8.0 and 0.8.1 in one step. 0.7 and 0.8.0 were not released on their own: we kept building and testing, and one bigger, well-tested step is easier on everyone than many quick ones. If you played 0.6.5, this is what is new for you.

**Since 0.6.5, in short**
- **One number for each question.** A total is your character's whole count, with its parts under it. Press Y to see what it holds and from when.
- **One look across the book.** Each part of a page sits on its own island. Every bar has a short list under it with name, number and share. Every Deeds page has time periods, from 10 minutes to All.
- **Everyone.** One chip in the player row shows the page you are on for the whole group: the total first, then each player's share.
- **Battle, in depth.** Damage by weapon, type and foe, and what hurt you in the same rows; what your armor stopped per damage type beside it. By foe shows how many foes of each kind you fought and defeated.
- **Feed and Last fight.** The feed tells your fights this session, foe by foe, with filters for time, biome and foe. Last fight shows what you dealt together with the players near you.
- **Recent and While away.** Recent shows what grew lately. After a day or more away, the book opens on what your fellow players did meanwhile.
- **Group feats.** Feats > Together has feats the whole group earns, with who helped and each one's share.
- **Meals.** A new Deeds page shows the meals you ate, dish by dish, and with Everyone on, who cooked for whom.
- **Compare.** Set today beside yesterday, or the last 7 days beside the 7 before, part by part.
- **Fellow players, sooner.** Their pages show from their last shared copy as soon as you log in. With 0.8.1 on the server, their numbers move about every 10 seconds.
- **Credit where it belongs.** A feast credits both the one who made it and the one who set it out. Materials from a piece that came down count as recovered, not as gathered.
- **Safer saves.** Your files keep a backup copy, so a crash or power cut loses nothing from before the last save. A renamed character keeps its numbers.

**Server.** Client and server are one file, as before. A 0.8.1 client works on a server that still runs Hearthwoven 0.6. Put 0.8.1 on the server too for the live updates every 10 seconds; without it, fellow players' numbers refresh every 2 minutes, and everything else works the same. Two fixes also need 0.8.1 on the server: a world made again under the same name, and two players whose characters share one id.

Below: everything new in 0.8.1, then the full 0.8.0 and 0.7.0 lists.

- **Calmer pages.** Each part of a page sits on its own soft gray island: a bar with its legend, a ranking, a list. No divider lines.
- **One legend grid.** A legend sits under its bar, and every legend on a page uses the same columns, four where they fit, so they line up. Bars are a little thinner; the wood grain and groundwork bars stay tall.
- **One kind of heading.** Every heading on a page looks the same. Headings show their total where the parts add up.
- **One hero.** Every page shows its big number the same way, a little smaller with less room above it; the growth line sits on its right, over the skill, and never makes the page taller. Point at it for its dates.
- **One page head.** The title strip is darker, and About these numbers always sits at its right end. Nothing loose sits above or beside the numbers: why a time chip is grayed shows when you point at it (or under it when you press it); "Earlier counts may be incomplete." and the rest are in About these numbers. A fellow player's book says whose copy and when at the strip's right end.
- **Room on group pages.** Everyone pages fit one screen; on Recent each player has their own island, up to six gains in two columns.
- **One damage row.** Battle > Damage (By weapon, By type and By foe), Defense's "Received from" and Last fight's foes now share one compact row per weapon, damage type or foe: every bar on one scale but starting a quarter of the way along, so small rows still show what they are made of; a thin line under each bar shows its true length; numbers sit inside the parts, and the shares are on one line under the bar. By type splits each damage type by Melee, Bow and Magic. Point at a part too narrow for its number to see it. The weapon skills are in the Weapon skills strip on every Damage view.
- **The grayed Everyone chip says where it works.** On a page where it cannot show the group (Skills, Feats, Company, Battle's Feed and Last fight, and a few overviews), the chip says where it does work ("on Damage, Defense"); a press, or the book key, opens the nearest such page with the group shown.
- **Recent with Everyone.** On Deeds > Recent the Everyone chip shows the group's Recent; Recent's own You / The group switch is gone. Each player gets up to six gains, and nothing is cut off.
- **New page Deeds > Meals.** The meals you have eaten, dish by dish with the most first, each in the dish's own color. With Everyone on, By dish splits each of the group's dishes by who cooked it, and By player splits each player's meals by whose cooking it was, so who fed whom shows at a glance. Food with no cook on it, picked, found or gathered, shows as Gathered; a feast counts for the one who made it. Today, 7 days and 30 days work on your own page.
- **The book key and a grayed Everyone.** Where Everyone is grayed, B (or the controller's Y) opens the nearest page where it works, instead of skipping it.
- **Sort both ways.** Click the chosen order again to turn it round: Most becomes Least, A-Z becomes Z-A.
- **Filter the battle feed.** Battle > Feed has the time chips (10 min to Session) and Biome and Foe filters (press K). It looks through every fight the book still keeps this session, up to 200 foes, so an early Gjall is found after 48 Roots. A fight shows only its foes that match, says how many ("1 of 5 foes") and adds up their damage. The window and filters are kept, like the view.
- **Faster page turns.** The book keeps its tabs, page list and player row when you turn a page, and reuses its text and pictures instead of making new ones. It also gets ready while it is still closed after you spawn, so the first opening stalls less.
- **The book on top.** The ship's controls, the hotbar and other mods' HUD no longer draw over the open book. The book closes when one of the game's own windows opens (a popup, the menu, the console, a trader).
- **The feed as damage bars.** In Battle > Feed, the Log and the Cards show what you dealt as a damage bar with the numbers inside, on one scale for the whole feed. What you received stays as short words.
- **Small numbers say <1.** An amount that rounds to nothing reads <1, a share <1 %.
- **US spelling.** The book now writes armor, defense, color and gray, as Valheim does.
- **Measured in the game.** On one player's PC at 4K, with a real character and Everyone off: switching to another page takes about 11 ms for a typical page. With the book open and nothing changing, it costs about 0.03 ms per frame. The first opening after the game starts takes about 60 ms.
- **Known:** pages with long lists turn slower. Deeds > Building draws every kind of piece you built and can take up to about 0.2 seconds on a large build (365 kinds on our test character); Cooking and Crafting take up to about 75 ms. We are working on it.

## 0.8.0 (not released on its own; part of 0.8.1)
Every page for the whole group with one chip, each foe and each fight in Battle, and feasts that credit both hands. Client and server are one file, as before. A 0.8 client works on any server with Hearthwoven 0.6.0 or newer. The live updates every 10 seconds need 0.7 or newer on the server. Two fixes need 0.8 on the server itself: a world made again under the same name, and two players whose characters share one id.
- **Everyone in the player row.** A chip at the end of the player row turns the page you are on into the group's page: the group's total first, then a bar for each player with their share, then the page's own breakdown for everyone. The rows run you first, then the others by name, never a ranking. While the chip is on, each name in the player row gets a short line in its color, so the row is the key to the bars. Each row says how fresh its numbers are ("this PC", "live", "as of 8 Oct 00:10"); someone whose numbers cannot fill the chosen period is named and left out of the total, and someone who shares no totals counts with their last shared session, which the page says. It works on Woodcutting, Mining, Building, Groundwork, Crafting, Cooking, Farming, Fishing, Taming, Battle's Damage and Defense, Sailing, Cargo, On foot, Trader and Smelters. On Battle > Damage it adds By player, and By type shows the damage types down with a column for each player. Today, 7 days and 30 days work for damage dealt only: fellow players share days for nothing else, and the page says so. Where a sum means nothing (Skills, Feats, Company, About, Recent, the overviews, Battle's Deaths, Foes, Feed and Last fight, and Maps) the chip is grayed and says why. The book remembers the chip between sessions.
- **Battle > Damage, By foe.** A third view beside By weapon and By type: every kind of foe you hit, with how many separate foes of that kind you fought (shown as ×3; a troll you hit fifty times is one foe), a mark for each (filled: defeated, alone or with fellow players; ring: got away; gold ring: still fighting) and your damage by type on one scale. Past 30 foes of a kind, words take over from the marks ("412 defeated · 88 got away"). Foes are counted from the day you first play 0.8; on All and the long periods the list says "counts from" that day. Counts are kept for all biomes together, so with a biome chosen the page shows no counts and says why. A fellow player's book shows the same marks, still fighting included. In This session their ×N and marks show only when everything they did this session falls inside yours; when they were playing before you came in, the counts show in All, and the page says so.
- **Battle > Defense** shows the same ×N after each foe: how many foes of that kind hit you.
- **New page Battle > Feed.** Your fights this session, newest first. Each foe says what became of it, "defeated by you", "defeated with Edda", "survived" or "got away", with the damage you dealt and received, by type. Three views: Log, Cards and Timeline; the book remembers your choice. A fight ends after a minute without hits. The page draws the newest 40 foes, and one line says what is left out. The book keeps your newest 200 foes of the session; past that, one line says the older fights are no longer kept, and a fight it holds only part of is left out (unless it is the only one). The feed stays on your PC for this session: fellow players never see it.
- **New page Battle > Last fight.** Your last fight with the fellow players near you: the damage you dealt together, each player's part, the damage by type, the foes you fought (with ×N and the marks) and what you received. "Earlier fights" opens the feed. A player is in the fight when they were within 40 m of you for 10 seconds or more while hits were coming, or hit one of its foes with you. Your PC works out who was near from the players the game has loaded around you; nothing new is shared. Fellow players' parts come from the minutes their shared copies hold, so up to a minute at each end of the fight can fall in or out. A fellow player who joined again after the fight shows without numbers and is left out of the sums.
- **Feasts credit both hands.** When you set out a feast someone else made, your PC notes who made it. Fireside then runs the thread from the one who made it to the one who enjoyed it, and a line names the other hand ("Tor set it out · × 3 enjoyed"), marked as teamwork. If the maker is not around the fire, the feast stays with the one who set it out and the line says who made it ("Edda made it"). Food shared says it under the row ("Teamwork: 3 servings from a feast Tor set out"); Cooking's "Who enjoyed your food" and While away count the same way. The maker is named as the game writes it on the item, also when they do not use Hearthwoven; they get no numbers, only their name on the line. Feasts set out before 0.8 keep "set out": the game does not remember who made them. Nothing is written to the world.
- **Compare.** A Compare chip after the time periods sets this period beside the one before it: Today against yesterday, or 7 days against the 7 days before. The big numbers show the number before and how much it changed ("▲ 12 %", "▼ 30 %", "new", "same"). Every bar opens into one row per part on one scale: this period solid, the one before as an outline at its length, then now, before and the change. The mark never judges: no green, no red. It works on the Deeds pages with time periods (not Overview or Recent), Battle's Damage and Defense, Sailing, Cargo, and Damage dealt on Company > Together, with a row for each player. Compare works on Today and 7 days only. 30 days is grayed: your PC keeps single days for 35 days, so it cannot cut out the 30 days before exactly. It is also grayed on 10 minutes to Session and on All (there is no earlier stretch of minutes to set beside them), in a fellow player's book (they share totals, not days), and until your day history reaches far enough; point at the chip to see when it opens ("Compare opens on 14 Oct."). On Defense, what your armor stopped stays this period only, and the page says so. The book remembers whether Compare is on. With Everyone on it is grayed too, except on Battle > Damage, where it compares the group player by player, from each fellow player's shared days. On By foe it compares the damage per kind of foe; the ×N and the marks show with Compare off.
- **Growth lines.** Above the big number on Woodcutting, Mining, Cooking, Building, Groundwork, Crafting, Farming and Fishing, and with damage dealt on Battle > Overview (all biomes): a small gold column for each of the last 14 days, from your day history. The last seven add up to 7 days; the last one is Today. Only the days your history holds are drawn, never a 0 before them. A fellow player's Deeds pages show the place grayed ("no days shared"); their damage dealt comes from the days they share.
- **Point at a bar part** and it gets a gold ring while its row in the list lights up; point at a row and its part lights. Nothing moves. A thin sliver is easy to find from its row. On While away, a player's row lights the total it belongs to.
- **More skills beside their numbers.** Battle > Damage, By weapon: each weapon carries its weapon skills, the one with the most damage first (at most 3). Defense: Blocking beside blocks and parries. Sailing: a mod's sailing skill beside the big number, when a mod adds one (the game has none).
- **Battle > Foes by keys and controller.** A/D, the arrow keys or the D-pad move between foes; Enter, Space or the A button opens or closes a foe's ranking of arrows, bolts and weapons, and the chosen row shows the exact damage factors. The page scrolls just far enough to show it.
- **Materials recovered.** Materials you pick up from a piece that came down, whether your hammer took it down, another player's did or a monster broke it, count as "Materials recovered" on Deeds > Building, not as wood, stone or ore brought in on Woodcutting and Mining. Counts from before 0.8 cannot be corrected; the About boxes of Woodcutting and Mining say from when. Ruins you break still count as brought in: their materials are new to the world.
- **Page Up and Page Down** scroll every page, as the wheel does, and so does the controller's right stick.
- **Whose book, by keys and controller.** B, or the controller's Y, steps through the player row: you, each fellow player, then Everyone, as a click on a name does. A player who is just joining, and Everyone where it is grayed, are skipped. The key is the setting Panel.BookKey; on a full key line it is the first to step aside after the scroll hint.
- **A page that can't be drawn never locks the book.** If something goes wrong while a page is drawn, the book shows "This page could not be drawn" and keeps the tabs, the list and the player row working, so you can open another page or turn Everyone off; the full error goes to the log once. Also fixed: Everyone on a time period where nobody could be counted no longer fails. If an error closes the book, it opens again on Deeds > Overview with Everyone off.
- **Fixed:** Everyone on Building no longer stops the book when a player's hammer has more tabs than a bar shows; the book no longer writes a font warning for every label.
- **Fixed:** pointing at a bar part works across the whole page. In the middle of the page, bar parts did not light up (Building, Crafting, Woodcutting): the game's own hover text, empty while the book is open, still caught the pointer there. The book now takes the pointer first wherever it is drawn.
- **Fixed:** Since you were away says it plainly: "You were away 3 days, since Thursday 21:40", or "since 11:05 today" when it was today.
- **Fixed:** Battle's overview on Today, 7 days or 30 days with nothing in it shows the biome tiles under its line, as This session does.
- **Safer files.** Your filter choices, and the book's other choices (the Everyone chip, Compare, the feed's view), are kept per character with a backup copy, like your totals. When a file was cut short by a crash or power loss, your filter choices and what your PC last saw of fellow players (for Recent and While away) come back from the copy before, and the log names the file.
- **A renamed character keeps its numbers.** A character renamed with a save editor takes over its earlier file once; the old file is kept. If more than one earlier file could fit, none is taken, never merged.
- **Going back from a newer version** keeps that version's numbers: 0.8 saves the parts it does not know along with its own, and never saves over a newer file of fellow players' marks or of your choices.
- **Server: one book per world.** A world made again under the same name starts its own book (cargo loaded and unloaded, young born near you) instead of carrying on the old world's. A renamed world keeps its book. The book from before 0.8 goes once to the world that is running, and the old file is kept.
- **Server: two players, one character id.** Two players whose characters share one id (a character file given to a friend, or one character played from a Steam and an Xbox account) are kept apart: each gets their own numbers and their own shared copy.
- **Measured in the game.** On one player's PC at 4K, with a real character: with the book open and nothing changing, the book costs about 0.04 ms and 45 bytes of memory per frame, close to nothing next to a game frame of about 12 ms. The update every 2 seconds takes about 2 ms. Switching to another page takes about 18 ms for a typical page, a frame or two, and up to about 65 ms for Cooking, the slowest. The first time Recent opened, one frame took 0.4 seconds. We are working on faster page switches for 0.8.1. A fellow player's 10-second update is now read once, on a background thread, instead of three times on the game's own.
- Developer tool **Dev.Bench** (off for players): with it on, F4 (Dev.BenchKey) or a `bench-request` file in `BepInEx/Hearthwoven/local/` flips through every page a few times (Dev.BenchRounds, 5) and writes what each costs to `BepInEx/Hearthwoven/local/bench-<version>.json`. **Dev.SelfCheck**'s one-minute performance line also says the bytes per frame, the phases of a rebuild and the garbage collections.
- Developer setting **Battle.DealtAfterArmour** (off by default): measures your damage after the foe's resistances and armor, on the PC that controls the foe. A test, not a feature: nothing in the book shows it yet, and it is never saved or shared. It takes effect at the next game start.

## 0.7.0 (not released on its own; part of 0.8.1)
One number for each question, a page for what grew lately, a page for what happened while you were away, and group feats. Client and server are one file, as before. A 0.7 client works on any server with Hearthwoven 0.6.0 or newer. Only the live updates every 10 seconds need 0.7 on the server too; without it, the book refreshes every 2 minutes instead and everything else works the same.
- **One number for each question.** A page no longer splits a number into a faded "before install" part and a "since install" part. A total is your character's whole count where the game keeps one, with its parts under it. What only Hearthwoven counts says from which day, in your own date: "Recorded from 8 October · this PC". A number nobody recorded says "Not recorded", never 0. Where an earlier count may miss something, the page says so once: "Earlier counts may be incomplete."
- **About these numbers.** A button on the page, or **Y**, opens a short box: what the numbers hold before that day, what they hold from it, and any detail worth knowing. The longer story stays in About (T).
- **Your skill beside the big number** on Cooking, Crafting, Farming and Fishing too, as on Woodcutting and Mining: "Fishing · level 21", at the right end of the row with the big number.
- **Deeds > Recent: what grew lately.** The same periods as Battle, from 10 minutes to All. You: what you brought in, made, built, planted and felled, biggest first, and the skills you practiced. The group: one row for each player who shares, in alphabetical order, never a ranking. For fellow players: this session and All, the short periods when the server sends live updates; their days are not shared, and the page says so.
- **Since you were away.** The first time you open the book after a day or more away, it opens on Company > While away: what your fellow players did meanwhile, the group's totals first, then each player's share, whose food they enjoyed and whose gear they put to good use. It is told from what each of them last shared. Once per session; turn it off with **ShowWhileAway**, or set the hours with **WhileAwayHours**.
- **Battle > Defense: Received or Your armor.** A switch (F) shows Received, everything that hurt you (the default), or Your armor: each hit as it reached your armor and as it left it, per damage type and per foe. Counted on your PC from the first day you play 0.7, and not shared.
- **Defense > Received from per damage type:** how much of each damage type each foe dealt you, on your own book and on fellow players' books. The foes' bars share one scale, so you can compare them at a glance.
- **Battle > Foes: what to bring.** Under each damage type a small meter shows how much of it the foe takes: half full is normal, full is twice as much, empty with a cross is none. Point at a meter for the exact factor. Click a foe on your own book to rank arrows, bolts and weapons against it, the best and worst three of each, every one marked owned, made, can craft or not known yet. Base damage only: upgrades and skill are not counted.
- **Group feats.** Feats > Together has eight new feats the whole group earns, such as The Hall Well Fed (dishes cooked) and Clad for the Road (armor crafted). Everyone who shares adds to one number, and each character's own record counts, in every world. The detail shows who helped and each one's share ("Who cooked: Edda 200 · 71 %"), and every tier of a feat shows as a small mark, so you can see how far it goes before you earn the first. A feat that belongs to a land shows once someone who shares has found that land, some of them first as a riddle. Iron for the Forge now waits for its land too. The targets come from a real group's play so far and may still change. The Charcoal Burners counts the coal you take, so a kiln a Stoker's Chest feeds counts too. On a feat that adds your record to what Hearthwoven counted, each player's book can show a slightly different total.
- **Stoker's Chest feeding shows apart.** Hall > Smelters shows what a Stoker's Chest fed as its own part, never as yours; the Forgekeeper's line counts what you fed by hand.
- **Time periods on every Deeds page:** 10 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour, 3 hours, this session, Today, 7 days, 30 days and All, with the filters working inside them. A Deeds page opens on All until you pick a period. On a fellow player's book the periods show grayed, with one line that says why. The Overview stays on All, because its titles are earned over all your play. Pieces placed per kind, crops picked per kind and animals tamed are kept per day from your first 0.7 session.
- **Sort a list:** Most, A-Z or By category above the lists on Building, Crafting (by kind) and Fishing. Sorting only changes the order.
- **One bar form across the book:** rounded parts with small gaps, and under each bar a short list with name, number and share in %. Long lists sit in two columns; every part keeps its own row and its own sliver in the bar, and a tiny one says "less than 1 %", never 0. An item keeps its own color and wood keeps its grain; a category such as a hammer tab keeps one color on every page. Damage-type bars carry their numbers inside. A closed filter on Building, Crafting and Cooking still shows its first bar.
- **Fireside:** thinner lines for small counts and larger arrowheads, so the direction of a gift is easy to see. The list beside the fire says once per section where its numbers were counted, each row on one line above its arrow. A feast is set out, never made: its servings get a row of their own ("set out a Mountains feast · × 3 enjoyed"). A player is never credited with more of a dish than they made, and a craft that makes several (4 sausages, 4 Queen's Jam) counts as all of them.
- **Fellow players show sooner.** Someone who just joined shows at once as a dimmed "just joined" until their book arrives. Your first update goes 10 seconds after you spawn (was 30), then every 2 minutes (was 5). A config still on the old default of 5 moves to 2 once; if you choose 5 again, it stays.
- **Fellow players show at once after you log in.** Your PC keeps each fellow player's last shared copy for that world, so their pages show straight away, marked "as Edda last shared it", until the server's fresh copy arrives. The copies, and a small file of their running totals for Recent and While away, are plain files on your PC, per world, for up to 14 days; switching sharing off deletes them, and a fellow who leaves the server's list is deleted too.
- **This session means your session.** A fellow player's numbers in This session count only what they did while you were playing, on Recent, Battle and Together; someone who was not on says so ("Edda has not played since 21:40."). A fellow player's blocks and parries are kept for their whole session, so on Defense they show in All only, and the page says so.
- **Live updates every 10 seconds** (needs 0.7 on the server). Between full updates, each player who shares sends only what changed, a few hundred bytes. With the book open you see fellow players' numbers move while you play. The server keeps only the latest small update for each player, in memory. Your fellow players then see what you do, the land you fight in included, about every 10 seconds instead of every few minutes: the same numbers as before, sooner. A fellow player still on 0.6.5 sends every 5 minutes, so their numbers move at that pace, and Recent names them ("Edda's Hearthwoven is older: choose Session.").
- **The lands you found** now travel with your shared copy, so a group feat's land counts for everyone who shares. Still never a place.
- **Titles page** uses its full height, and "More below" no longer covers a card. A title you hold only through the game's own count says so ("Stone and ore broken before 9 October").
- **New pictures** for Recent, While away, Titles and Many Crafts, One Hall, and line pictures for the totals on While away.

## 0.6.5 (2026-10-09, client release)
From the first evening with the Thunderstore install. Client only: the server stays on 0.6.0.
- **Your skill is back** on Woodcutting and Mining. Pages with time windows had stopped drawing it (the heading row held the windows); it now sits at the top of the page ("Wood Cutting · level 34").
- **Titles explained.** Feats has a Titles page: what a title is (a kind of work you do) and what a feat is (a moment you reach), every title you hold with its reason, the others greyed with what earns them. On a page a title says why ("Wallwarden · 3 defences built, armed or loaded") and opens its entry; it shows once (it was drawn twice in game) and not inside a short time window.
- **Calmer time windows.** No standing "Day history since…" line on every page: press a greyed 7 days or 30 days and one line says from when it works. An empty window says one thing, not three.
- **Keys.** Tab closes the book, as it closes your inventory; the filter key moves to K. An existing config that still has the old default Tab is switched to K once; if you choose Tab again, it stays.
- **Cooking has filters**: Type (meals, grilled, baked; feasts and mead bases when made) and Main boost (health, stamina, eitr).
- **Bars you can read.** A filter or composition bar shows at most eight parts in clearly different colours, the rest as "Other (n kinds)"; "Misc" and "Misc." are one category; the cultivator's pieces from PlantEverything count as plantings, not as buildings with "Beech Seeds" as material.
- **Mining** counts stone and ore: Leather Scraps are out (boars drop them too), Withered Bone and Chitin show apart as "Other pickaxe finds".
- **Smaller:** an amount between 0 and 1 says "under 1" instead of 0; a hit your ward took completely is no hit received; deaths keep up to the last 30 seconds of damage (the docs said 10).
- **A greyed window stays where you pressed it.** The "7 days works from…" line shows on that page only; another page, another chapter or reopening the book goes back to the last window that worked (Battle keeps its Session). The same on Company > Together.
- **One block count on Defence.** Shieldbearer's reason splits blocks and parries as the numbers below it do ("254 blocks · 58 parries").
- **Cooking's Other** sits last in its bars, in the quiet colour, as on Crafting and Building.
- **A hit your ward took completely** is no hit received in the short windows either (10 min to Session).
- **Modded tools:** a tool that holds buildings with a seed and a hoe among them no longer counts every building as planted.
- **Titles that do not fit** the strip at a page's top end in "+2 more", which opens the Titles page.

## 0.6.1 (2026-10-09, client release)
The client build of the 0.6 line. It needs a server on **0.6.0 or newer** to show what only the server counts (cargo loaded and unloaded, born near you); without one, everything else works and those two are left out.

## Next: server resilience (RESILIENCE-06, server side; not released)
- Server files are written whole: the per-player JSON, every session copy, the share copies and the server book go to a temp file flushed to disk, then replace the old one, which stays as `.bak`. An unreadable book falls back to its `.bak`.
- Input limits for snapshots: parts and messages are bounded, half-sent messages are dropped after 2 minutes (no slow memory leak), at most one snapshot per player per 20 seconds, at most 4 MB unpacked (a gzip bomb stops there), only one well-formed JSON object is stored. Each problem is logged once per player.
- The cargo book is rebuilt per world: the chest log markers name the world, so a new world never starts with the old one's cargo.

- Routed damage logging (`Server.LogRoutedDamage`) is off by default (Joost). A server config that already has it on keeps it on until changed.
