# Changelog

Every tweak that reaches a PC or the server gets its own patch version, so the version in the
log line and in Gale always says which build runs.

## 0.10.0 (2026-10-21, client release)
- **Boss fights:** a boss fight's head no longer draws the boss's name over the big numbers; with big numbers they step down a size or move to their own line.
- **Dev: live reload** (`Dev.LiveReload`, off for players): the book can be rebuilt in the running game with ScriptEngine; a reload saves everything on the PC, continues as after a reconnect and reopens the book on its page, scrolled as it was (0.9.12), and sends nothing: the server gets the whole connection as one session after the logout (README "Live reload").
- **Armor sets count what you wore too.** A piece counts when you made it or put it on yourself, whoever made it. On Armor sets a gold dot marks made, a ring marks worn, and the key under the number says from which day wearing is counted (what you wore before you install this is not known). Clad for Every Land counts the same way.
- **Clad as One**, a new Together feat: every armor set of a land complete across the group, each piece made or worn by someone who shares (1 land, half the lands, every land). With Everyone on, Armor sets shows who made or wore a piece of each set. A friend on an older version adds only the pieces they made.

## 0.8.6 (2026-10-15, client and server release)
This book now has a new name on Thunderstore: **Hearthwoven Stats Book**. The plugin, your settings and your saved book are the same. This release adds damage per second (DPS) for each weapon, a Feed that reaches 30 days back, and a friend's older fights, which your PC fetches from the server. Put 0.8.6 on the server and on your friends' PCs too: a friend's older Feed, and the faster fetching of it, need it. With an older server or an older PC, those parts stay as they were. Your own book works on any server.

**New name.** On Thunderstore the book is now the package Hearthwoven Stats Book (`Hearthwoven_Stats_Book`). The old package, Hearthwoven, gets this version one more time, with a note that it moved, and is then deprecated. Remove the old one in your mod manager and install the new one. Keep only one: they hold the same plugin (the same ID), so you do not want both enabled. Your settings and your saved book stay, because they do not belong to the package.

Works with Valheim 1.0.20 (the patch of 15 October), checked on a PC and on a dedicated server.

**In short**
- **DPS for each weapon.** On Battle > Damage > By weapon, with the newest DPS and the trend.
- **Open a weapon.** A weapon with several upgrade levels is one line. Press it to see each level and each skill level.
- **A Feed that reaches 30 days back.** Show older foes loads the next fights. A friend's older Feed comes from the server.
- **Runs and bosses has time windows.** It also has plainer words and the real name of each dungeon.
- **Your pace in a fight.** Your DPS in each fight, and the group's pace in a boss fight with the time left.
- **Easier to use.** A Back button, a strip at the top of a page that stays up, and a hover tag that does not hide the bar.

**Battle: weapons and pace**
- **DPS for each weapon.** On Battle > Damage > By weapon, each weapon shows its damage per second (DPS), for example "Melee 38 DPS". It is your damage divided by the seconds you fought, not by the seconds on the clock. A pause of more than 4 seconds between your hits does not count, and the first hit after a pause starts the clock. With less than 5 seconds of fighting, the line shows a dash. About these numbers says all this. DPS shows on your own book and on a fellow player's book, never on Everyone, with a Biome choice or in Compare. Hearthwoven counts the fighting time from this version on, so older damage has no seconds. A window that reaches back before then counts only from the day it began, and All says that day.
- **Each weapon and its upgrade level.** Under Melee, Bow and Magic, By weapon lists the weapons you swung. A weapon that you used at more than one upgrade level is one line at its newest level, for example "Mistwalker 1 711 · level 3 · 149 DPS at 35 · 2 levels", with a › at its end. Press it (or move to it with A/D and press Enter, or the controller's A) and its upgrade levels open in place, each with its damage, DPS and skill level, such as "Frostner · level 3 · 42 DPS · Clubs 38–45". What you opened stays open while you stay on the page and the window. The rows of Melee, Bow and Magic keep their totals, and the weapon lines under them add up to the row: hits from before weapons were counted, and hits on players, are one last line, Not by weapon. A hit with no weapon of its skill in your hand (an arrow that lands after you changed weapon) goes to Other weapons. Each kind shows at most 8 weapons. Hearthwoven records weapons from this version on; About these numbers says the day they count from.
- **The newest DPS on a weapon line.** Your skill rises while you play, so the average of a window mixes old and new skill levels. A line shows the DPS at the highest skill level that has at least 5 seconds of fighting in the window, and says that level when it is not the highest ("46 DPS at 26 · Spears 22–27"). After it a small arrow shows the change against the lowest skill level that reached 5 seconds: "▲ 6" for 6 more DPS, "▼ 1" for 1 less. There is no arrow when only one level reaches 5 seconds or the DPS did not change. When no level reaches 5 seconds, the line shows the DPS of the whole window, or a dash.
- **Each skill level of a weapon.** A weapon line with two skill levels or more opens, and an upgrade level with several skill levels opens further. It shows one row for each skill level (the newest 12), such as "Spears 24 · 45 DPS", with a small bar on one scale, so you see the trend. A level with less than 5 seconds of fighting shows a dash and no bar. Hearthwoven keeps the seconds and the damage for each whole skill level: up to 4 levels for a minute, 8 for a day, 12 for a week or a month, always the highest ones. The totals always hold every hit.
- **A fellow player's weapons.** A fellow player's copy now carries their weapons for each minute and for each day of the last 30 days: each weapon at each upgrade level, with its fighting seconds, its damage and the lowest and highest level of its skill (at most 12 weapons a minute and a day). So their By weapon shows the same lines as yours, in 10 min to 3 h, Session, Today, 7 days and 30 days, and their weapon lines open the same way. Their All shows none, and a copy from before 0.8.6 shows none and says so. The copy grows by about 0.9 KB (packed) for an evening of an hour of fighting, about 1.6 KB for a typical month and about 7 KB for a very busy one. A live update of a busy fight stays under 250 bytes.
- **Your pace in a fight.** Battle > Last fight and each fight of the Feed show your own DPS in that fight, beside dealt and received ("your DPS" on Last fight, "38 DPS" in the Feed). It counts the time between your own hits with any weapon. A fight with less than 5 seconds of your fighting shows a dash, and a fight from before this version shows nothing. Your kept fights keep it after a restart.
- **The group's pace in a boss fight.** The Boss fight page has one more number beside dealt and received: the DPS of the group on the boss, for the seconds the boss was under attack. While the fight is on, the health lane also says how long is left at the pace of the last minute, for example "64 % left · at this pace about 8:00 left". It says nothing when the boss lost less than 1 % in the last minute or more than 30 minutes are left. The page has no pace for a single player.

**The Feed**
- **The Feed reaches 30 days back.** Your book now keeps your fights of earlier sessions for up to 30 days, and your newest 2000 foes in each world. Before, it kept 7 days and 200 foes. So the Feed's Today, 7 days and 30 days, and Last fight, show the fights you kept. After a busy month (150 foes on five evenings a week), the newest 2000 foes reach about 19 days back. The file of kept fights is about 0.3 MB with no fellow players in it and about 0.7 MB with three, and the book reads it in about 40 ms when you start. A book on 0.8.5 can read the same file: it shows its own 200 foes and 7 days of it.
- **Show older foes.** At the end of Battle > Feed, the button Show older foes (or Enter, Space or the controller's A) draws the next fights below the ones on the page, whole, about 40 foes for each press. The line beside it says how many fights and foes are shown and how many are left in the period, such as "Shown: 7 fights, 36 of 110 foes in this window. Not shown: 3 earlier fights (74 foes)." A fight is never cut in half. A new period, filter, page or player starts at the first page again.
- **The Feed says the day of each fight.** A fight's head adds its day after the time: "Fight at 20:46" for today, "Fight at 20:46 yesterday", "Fight at 20:46 on Thursday" for 2 to 6 days back, and "Fight at 20:46 on 3 Oct" for older fights. The empty Feed names its window properly: "No fight today" and "No fight in the last 10 min".
- **A fellow player's Feed reaches 30 days back.** Before, the Feed of a fellow player showed only the fights of their running session. Now their Today, 7 days, 30 days and All open too. Their PC sends each session that has ended to the server once, a small file (about 5 KB for a busy evening). The server keeps the newest 60 sessions of each player in each world, up to 30 days back and no more than 2000 foes. Show older foes on their Feed gets their next session from the server, one at a time, and your PC keeps it for next time. The line under the Feed says how many earlier sessions are still on the server, such as "Not shown: 3 earlier sessions on the server (420 foes)". If one does not arrive, the button says Try again. A session holds the fights as the player's own PC kept them: no hits and no places, and a name only for players who share. It is deleted when the player stops sharing. With an older server or an older PC of a friend, their Feed stays as it was.
- **A friend's session shows that it is coming.** While a session of a fellow player is on its way, its line counts the seconds and says which one it is, for example "Getting Edda's session of Sunday, 20:25 to 22:30 from the server · 1 of 2 · 6 s". Only that line changes each second, not the page. The server sends a session in turns with the updates it is already sending to the group, so it does not wait until those are out. This needs 0.8.6 on the server. Your PC's log gets one line when it asks for a session and one when it arrives, with its size and the milliseconds in between.

**Runs and bosses, and fight pages**
- **Time windows on Runs and bosses.** Battle > Runs and bosses has the same window buttons as the other Battle pages: 10 min to 3 h, Session, Today, 7 days, 30 days and All. A run or a boss fight shows when any part of it is in the window, also when it started before the window and ended inside. A boss fight in a run follows its run. The fight that is on now always stays on top. When nothing is in the window, the page says so and tells you how long ago the newest run or boss fight was. The page opens on All, and the book remembers your choice.
- **Runs have the name of their dungeon and plainer words.** The game has no name label for a Burial Chamber, a Frost Cave or an Infested Mine, so most runs showed as "A dungeon". A run now gets its name and its land from the place where you went in (also the runs that your PC kept before this version). A run says "Left, 1 death" or "Inside now, 1 death", its page says "Left after 26 min", its time line "went in 21:02" and "left 21:20", and the players' column "Time inside". A visit under a minute with no fighting and no deaths is not on the list. It stays in the record.
- **The top island of a fight page shows the fight.** In 0.8.5 the top island of Last fight, a boss fight and a run showed only the name and a dot. Now it shows the boss or the door of the dungeon, its land, what the group dealt and received together, and the health of the boss or the time line of the run. For a fight that is on, the time says how long it has run in words ("Fighting now · 1 min 12 s", for a run "Inside now · 18 min"). In By player, a player who shares but hit none of the fight's foes says "did not hit these foes".
- **The players' table lines up.** In the By player island, the numbers of each column end at the right edge of the column's heading, also in the Deaths column. The skull is only in the heading of Deaths, and the small bar with the damage types of a fall stands before its number.
- **A friend's Last fight is the same page as yours.** On a fellow player's book, Battle > Last fight, Runs and bosses and each fight's own page now show the fight page of your own book, built from the same fights your PC joined, seen by them. They come first in the players' table as the book's owner, you are "Rowan (you)", "near Edda 30 s" under a player is their own count, and the head has their DPS ("12 Edda's DPS") when their copy carries it (from this build on, a few bytes for each fight). Before, their Last fight was the 0.8 page from their copy, which counted only the foes they hit themselves, so one fight said "18 foes defeated of 18" there and "all 31 foes defeated" on yours. When your PC holds no fight they were in, their Last fight shows the 0.8 page as before.
- **By type on every fight page.** Each fight's and run's page now ends with By type, together: the group's damage dealt by type in one bar, with the numbers in it, as the 0.8 page had it. It lies under the head, the foes and the players, so scroll down for it.

**Looking at damage**
- **The part the armor stopped is an outline.** On Battle > Defense > What your armor stopped, and on the bars of Battle > Damage and Foes, the part that got through is filled, with its number. The part that the armor stopped is only an outline with the dark track in it, as the key shows, and its number is in the hover tag. On Damage and Foes, a bar shows this only when armor was counted for all the damage of its window. A window with older damage shows plain bars and one line, for example "Armor counted from 9 October: choose 7 days or shorter to see what got through." A share above 100 % says why: "105 % got through (weak foes)", on a type's row "(weak to Fire)".
- **The hover tag sits beside its bar.** The name and the number of a part of a damage bar show under the bar, or above it when under would leave the scroll view or the bar's island. The tag is fully opaque, so the bar does not show through it. Before, the tag lay over the bar and hid the part you pointed at.

**Using the book**
- **A Back button.** A run or a boss fight that you open from Battle > Runs and bosses, and each other page that you open with a click on a row or a link, shows Back with its key [Backspace] at the left end of the strip at the top of the page. A click on Back does the same as Backspace. On a controller, B does the same on these pages. A page that you open from the list, with a tab or with a key has no Back button.
- **The strip at the top of a page stays up.** On every page that scrolls, the strip with its caption, the view buttons (Log, Cards and Timeline, By weapon, By type and By foe, and so on) and About these numbers stays at the top while the rest of the page scrolls under it. You no longer have to scroll all the way up to change the view. The strip has the same color and round corners as the islands under it. The rows go under its lower edge and never show through it, and a soft shadow under it shows that there is more above. A row that the keys move to stays in view under the strip. A page that does not scroll looks as before.

**Fixes**
- **Last fight is the newest fight you were in.** Before, it showed the newest fight of the whole group, so a fight of a fellow player that you were not in could replace yours, and their boss fight on turned the entry into "Boss fight" with the live dot. Now Last fight keeps your fight, and when a fellow player's fight is newer it ends with one quiet line, for example "Newer on the server: Edda fought a Seeker at 13:46 (you were not in it)". Press it to open that fight's own page, which says "You were not in this fight". With no fight of yours, Last fight shows its empty page and the same line.
- **A fellow player's older Feed shows when they have not fought yet.** When a fellow player had no fight in their running session, their Feed on 7 days, 30 days or All said "No fight in the last 7 days" and never got their earlier sessions from the server. Now it gets the newest one at once and says so, for example "Getting Edda's session of Sunday, 20:25 to 22:30 from the server", with Try again if it does not arrive. A window that their sessions do not reach says which window does, for example "Choose 7 days for Edda's earlier sessions."
- **A fellow player's empty Session keeps its page.** On the book of a fellow player who has not fought since you came in, Battle > Overview, Damage, Defense, Deaths and Foes in Session showed an older layout: the heading "Damage" instead of "Damage dealt", the window buttons on a row of their own, and no dark plate. Now the page looks as in the other windows and says "Nothing yet this session".

## 0.8.5 (2026-10-11, client and server release)
This is the big release after 0.8.3. 0.8.4 changed only the README. Put 0.8.5 on the server too: the server now counts what Stoker's Chests feed into smelters for the whole group, and only a server on 0.8.5 does this. Everything else also works on a server with 0.8.1. A fellow player on 0.8.4 or older keeps the new day periods grayed on your book, with one line that says why.

**In short**
- **Damage after the foe's armor.** Battle > Damage and Foes show what got through the foe's armor and resistances.
- **Your fights after a restart.** Last fight and the Feed keep your fights of earlier sessions, up to 7 days back.
- **Compare with a menu.** Compare against yesterday, the same day last week, or your best day.
- **Fellow players in every period.** Their Deeds, Sailing and Cargo pages, and Company > Together, open Today, 7 days and 30 days.
- **Denser pages.** Battle > Overview, While away and Together fit on one screen.
- **Stoker's Chest feeding is counted.** It was never counted before. Now the server counts it.

**Battle**
- **Damage after the foe's armor.** Battle > Damage (By weapon, By type and By foe) and Foes now show what got through the armor and resistances of the foe. Each bar is still the damage you swung. In each part, the filled piece is what got through and the outlined piece is what the armor of the foe stopped. The line under the bar ends with the share that got through, for example "73 % got through". When a foe is weak to a damage type, it takes more than you swung: that piece is all filled and the share is more than 100 %. Point at a part for its numbers. On Foes, click a foe to see your hits on it. Hearthwoven works this out on your PC at each hit, with the game's numbers for that foe, so it works for every foe, also in a group. It starts when you first play 0.8.5: a faded piece is damage from before then. Fellow players see yours in their short periods and Session.
- **One row per foe.** The game gives some different foes one name: the Draugr and the Draugr with a bow, the kinds of Skeleton, the Fuling and the Fuling archer. Each name is now one row, with the numbers of its kinds added up: on Battle > Overview, Damage by foe, Foes, Defense, the Feed and its Foe filter, Last fight, the fight pages and Compare, also with Everyone on. When the kinds of one name take damage differently, an open foe says that it shows the multipliers of the main kind.
- **Last fight and Feed keep your fights after a restart.** Battle > Last fight shows your last fight also when it was in an earlier session. The top says how long ago it was, such as "2 hours ago, at 22:31 · 2 min", and the page keeps the parts of the fellow players who shared that fight. The Feed shows the fights of your earlier sessions under the ones of this session, with a quiet line over each earlier session, such as "Yesterday, 20:05 to 23:40". Session shows the fights of this session only. 10 minutes to 3 hours, Today and the longer periods also show the earlier fights, and the filters work over all of them. When you have not fought yet in this session, the Feed opens on a period that shows your last fight. Your book keeps your newest 200 foes of earlier sessions for each world, up to 7 days back. A fellow player's copy still carries only the fights of their current session.
- **A player without numbers says why.** In the players' table of a fight, a fellow player who shares but has no numbers in that fight no longer reads "does not share". The line says what is true: their Hearthwoven is too old ("Edda's Hearthwoven is 0.8.2: Edda's part fills in once they update."), their newest copy came in before the fight, their copy no longer holds the fight, their copy starts after the fight because they reconnected, or their copy holds no hit on those foes.
- **Hildir's minibosses are boss fights.** Brenna, Geirrhafa, and Zil and Thungr get the Boss fight page and a row in Runs and bosses, also when the game does not mark them as bosses. Their names show as plain text, without the color codes the game puts in them.

## 0.8.0 (not released on its own; part of 0.8.1)
Every page for the whole group with one chip, each foe and each fight in Battle, and feasts that credit both hands. Client and server are one file, as before. A 0.8 client works on any server with Hearthwoven 0.6.0 or newer. The live updates every 10 seconds need 0.7 or newer on the server. Two fixes need 0.8 on the server itself: a world made again under the same name, and two players whose characters share one id.
- **Everyone in the player row.** A chip at the end of the player row turns the page you are on into the group's page: the group's total first, then a bar for each player with their share, then the page's own breakdown for everyone. The rows run you first, then the others by name, never a ranking. While the chip is on, each name in the player row gets a short line in its color, so the row is the key to the bars. Each row says how fresh its numbers are ("this PC", "live", "as of 8 Oct 00:10"); someone whose numbers cannot fill the chosen period is named and left out of the total, and someone who shares no totals counts with their last shared session, which the page says. It works on Woodcutting, Mining, Building, Groundwork, Crafting, Cooking, Farming, Fishing, Taming, Battle's Damage and Defense, Sailing, Cargo, On foot, Trader and Smelters. On Battle > Damage it adds By player, and By type shows the damage types down with a column for each player. Today, 7 days and 30 days work for damage dealt only: fellow players share days for nothing else, and the page says so. Where a sum means nothing (Skills, Feats, Company, About, Recent, the overviews, Battle's Deaths, Foes, Feed and Last fight, and Maps) the chip is grayed and says why. The book remembers the chip between sessions.
- **Battle > Damage, By foe.** A third view beside By weapon and By type: every kind of foe you hit, with how many separate foes of that kind you fought (shown as ×3; a troll you hit fifty times is one foe), a mark for each (filled: defeated, alone or with fellow players; ring: got away; gold ring: still fighting) and your damage by type on one scale. Past 30 foes of a kind, words take over from the marks ("412 defeated · 88 got away"). Foes are counted from the day you first play 0.8; on All and the long periods the list says "counts from" that day. Counts are kept for all biomes together, so with a biome chosen the page shows no counts and says why. A fellow player's book shows the same marks, still fighting included. In This session their ×N and marks show only when everything they did this session falls inside yours; when they were playing before you came in, the counts show in All, and the page says so.
- **Battle > Defense** shows the same ×N after each foe: how many foes of that kind hit you.
- **New page Battle > Feed.** Your fights this session, newest first. Each foe says what became of it, "defeated by you", "defeated with Edda", "survived" or "got away", with the damage you dealt and received, by type. Three views: Log, Cards and Timeline; the book remembers your choice. A fight ends after a minute without hits. The page draws the newest 40 foes, and one line says what is left out. The book keeps your newest 200 foes of the session; past that, one line says the older fights are no longer kept, and a fight it holds only part of is left out (unless it is the only one). The feed stays on your PC for this session: fellow players never see it.
- **New page Battle > Last fight.** Your last fight with the fellow players near you: the damage you dealt together, each player's part, the damage by type, the foes you fought (with ×N and the marks) and what you received. "Earlier fights" opens the feed. A player is in the fight when they were within 40 m of you for 10 seconds or more while hits were coming, or hit one of its foes with you. Your PC works out who was near from the players the game has loaded around you; nothing new is shared. Fellow players' parts come from the minutes their shared copies hold, so up to a minute at each end of the fight can fall in or out. A fellow player who joined again after the fight shows without numbers and is left out of the sums.
- **Feasts credit both hands.** When you set out a feast someone else made, your PC notes who made it. Fireside then runs the thread from the one who made it to the one who enjoyed it, and a line names the other hand ("Tor set it out · × 3 enjoyed"), marked as teamwork. If the maker is not around the fire, the feast stays with the one who set it out and the line says who made it ("Edda made it"). Food shared says it under the row ("Teamwork: 3 servings from a feast Tor set out"); Cooking's "Who enjoyed your food" and While away count the same way. The maker is named as the game writes it on the item, also when they do not use Hearthwoven; they get no numbers, only their name on the line. Feasts set out before 0.8 keep "set out": the game does not remember who made them. Nothing is written to the world.
- **Compare.** A Compare chip after the time periods sets this period beside the one before it: Today against yesterday, or 7 days against the 7 days before. The big numbers show the number before and how much it changed ("▲ 12 %", "▼ 30 %", "new", "same"). Every bar opens into one row per part on one scale: this period solid, the one before as an outline at its length, then now, before and the change. The mark never judges: no green, no red. It works on the Deeds pages with time periods (not Overview or Recent), Battle's Damage and Defense, Sailing, Cargo, and Damage dealt on Company > Together, with a row for each player. Compare works on Today and 7 days only. 30 days is grayed: your PC keeps single days for 35 days, so it cannot cut out the 30 days before exactly. It is also grayed on 10 minutes to Session and on All (there is no earlier stretch of minutes to set beside them), in a fellow player's book (they share totals, not days), and until your day history reaches far enough; point at the chip to see when it opens ("Compare opens on 14 Oct."). On Defense, what your armor stopped stays this period only, and the page says so. The book remembers whether Compare is on. With Everyone on it is grayed too, except on Battle > Damage, where it compares the group player by player, from each fellow player's shared days. On By foe it compares the damage per kind of foe; the ×N and the marks show with Compare off.
- **Growth lines.** Above the big number on Woodcutting, Mining, Cooking, Building, Groundwork, Crafting, Farming and Fishing, and with damage dealt on Battle > Overview (all biomes): a small gold column for each of the last 14 days, from your day history. The last seven add up to 7 days; the last one is Today. Only the days your history holds are drawn, never a 0 before them. A fellow player's Deeds pages show the place grayed ("no days shared"); their damage dealt comes from the days they share.
- **Point at a bar part** and it gets a gold ring while its row in the list lights up; point at a row and its part lights. Nothing moves. A thin sliver is easy to find from its row. On While away, a player's row lights the total it belongs to.
- **More skills beside their numbers.** Battle > Damage, By weapon: each weapon carries its weapon skills, the one with the most damage first (at most 3). Defense: Blocking beside blocks and parries. Sailing: a mod's sailing skill beside the big number, when a mod adds one (the game has none).
- **Battle > Foes by keys and controller.** A/D, the arrow keys or the D-pad move between foes; Enter, Space or the A button opens or closes a foe's ranking of arrows, bolts and weapons, and the chosen row shows the exact damage factors. The page scrolls just far enough to show it.
- **Materials recovered.** Materials you pick up from a piece that came down, whether your hammer took it down, another player's did or a monster broke it, count as "Materials recovered" on Deeds > Building, not as wood, stone or ore brought in on Woodcutting and Mining. Counts from before 0.8 cannot be corrected; the About boxes of Woodcutting and Mining say from when. Ruins you break still count as brought in: their materials are new to the world.
- **Page Up and Page Down** scroll every page, as the wheel does, and so does the controller's right stick.
- **Whose book, by keys and controller.** B, or the controller's Y, steps through the player row: you, each fellow player, then Everyone, as a click on a name does. A player who is just joining, and Everyone where it is grayed, are skipped. The key is the setting Panel.BookKey; on a full key line it is the first to step aside after the scroll hint.
- **A page that can't be drawn never locks the book.** If something goes wrong while a page is drawn, the book shows "This page could not be drawn" and keeps the tabs, the list and the player row working, so you can open another page or turn Everyone off; the full error goes to the log once. Also fixed: Everyone on a time period where nobody could be counted no longer fails. If an error closes the book, it opens again on Deeds > Overview with Everyone off.
- **Fixed:** Everyone on Building no longer stops the book when a player's hammer has more tabs than a bar shows; the book no longer writes a font warning for every label.
- **Fixed:** pointing at a bar part works across the whole page. In the middle of the page, bar parts did not light up (Building, Crafting, Woodcutting): the game's own hover text, empty while the book is open, still caught the pointer there. The book now takes the pointer first wherever it is drawn.
- **Fixed:** Since you were away says it plainly: "You were away 3 days, since Thursday 21:40", or "since 11:05 today" when it was today.
- **Fixed:** Battle's overview on Today, 7 days or 30 days with nothing in it shows the biome tiles under its line, as This session does.
- **Safer files.** Your filter choices, and the book's other choices (the Everyone chip, Compare, the feed's view), are kept per character with a backup copy, like your totals. When a file was cut short by a crash or power loss, your filter choices and what your PC last saw of fellow players (for Recent and While away) come back from the copy before, and the log names the file.
- **A renamed character keeps its numbers.** A character renamed with a save editor takes over its earlier file once; the old file is kept. If more than one earlier file could fit, none is taken, never merged.
- **Going back from a newer version** keeps that version's numbers: 0.8 saves the parts it does not know along with its own, and never saves over a newer file of fellow players' marks or of your choices.
- **Server: one book per world.** A world made again under the same name starts its own book (cargo loaded and unloaded, young born near you) instead of carrying on the old world's. A renamed world keeps its book. The book from before 0.8 goes once to the world that is running, and the old file is kept.
- **Server: two players, one character id.** Two players whose characters share one id (a character file given to a friend, or one character played from a Steam and an Xbox account) are kept apart: each gets their own numbers and their own shared copy.
- **Measured in the game.** On one player's PC at 4K, with a real character: with the book open and nothing changing, the book costs about 0.04 ms and 45 bytes of memory per frame, close to nothing next to a game frame of about 12 ms. The update every 2 seconds takes about 2 ms. Switching to another page takes about 18 ms for a typical page, a frame or two, and up to about 65 ms for Cooking, the slowest. The first time Recent opened, one frame took 0.4 seconds. We are working on faster page switches for 0.8.1. A fellow player's 10-second update is now read once, on a background thread, instead of three times on the game's own.
- Developer tool **Dev.Bench** (off for players): with it on, F4 (Dev.BenchKey) or a `bench-request` file in `BepInEx/Hearthwoven/local/` flips through every page a few times (Dev.BenchRounds, 5) and writes what each costs to `BepInEx/Hearthwoven/local/bench-<version>.json`. **Dev.SelfCheck**'s one-minute performance line also says the bytes per frame, the phases of a rebuild and the garbage collections.
- Developer setting **Battle.DealtAfterArmour** (off by default): measures your damage after the foe's resistances and armor, on the PC that controls the foe. A test, not a feature: nothing in the book shows it yet, and it is never saved or shared. It takes effect at the next game start.

## 0.7.0 (not released on its own; part of 0.8.1)
One number for each question, a page for what grew lately, a page for what happened while you were away, and group feats. Client and server are one file, as before. A 0.7 client works on any server with Hearthwoven 0.6.0 or newer. Only the live updates every 10 seconds need 0.7 on the server too; without it, the book refreshes every 2 minutes instead and everything else works the same.
- **One number for each question.** A page no longer splits a number into a faded "before install" part and a "since install" part. A total is your character's whole count where the game keeps one, with its parts under it. What only Hearthwoven counts says from which day, in your own date: "Recorded from 8 October · this PC". A number nobody recorded says "Not recorded", never 0. Where an earlier count may miss something, the page says so once: "Earlier counts may be incomplete."
- **About these numbers.** A button on the page, or **Y**, opens a short box: what the numbers hold before that day, what they hold from it, and any detail worth knowing. The longer story stays in About (T).
- **Your skill beside the big number** on Cooking, Crafting, Farming and Fishing too, as on Woodcutting and Mining: "Fishing · level 21", at the right end of the row with the big number.
- **Deeds > Recent: what grew lately.** The same periods as Battle, from 10 minutes to All. You: what you brought in, made, built, planted and felled, biggest first, and the skills you practiced. The group: one row for each player who shares, in alphabetical order, never a ranking. For fellow players: this session and All, the short periods when the server sends live updates; their days are not shared, and the page says so.
- **Since you were away.** The first time you open the book after a day or more away, it opens on Company > While away: what your fellow players did meanwhile, the group's totals first, then each player's share, whose food they enjoyed and whose gear they put to good use. It is told from what each of them last shared. Once per session; turn it off with **ShowWhileAway**, or set the hours with **WhileAwayHours**.
- **Battle > Defense: Received or Your armor.** A switch (F) shows Received, everything that hurt you (the default), or Your armor: each hit as it reached your armor and as it left it, per damage type and per foe. Counted on your PC from the first day you play 0.7, and not shared.
- **Defense > Received from per damage type:** how much of each damage type each foe dealt you, on your own book and on fellow players' books. The foes' bars share one scale, so you can compare them at a glance.
- **Battle > Foes: what to bring.** Under each damage type a small meter shows how much of it the foe takes: half full is normal, full is twice as much, empty with a cross is none. Point at a meter for the exact factor. Click a foe on your own book to rank arrows, bolts and weapons against it, the best and worst three of each, every one marked owned, made, can craft or not known yet. Base damage only: upgrades and skill are not counted.
- **Group feats.** Feats > Together has eight new feats the whole group earns, such as The Hall Well Fed (dishes cooked) and Clad for the Road (armor crafted). Everyone who shares adds to one number, and each character's own record counts, in every world. The detail shows who helped and each one's share ("Who cooked: Edda 200 · 71 %"), and every tier of a feat shows as a small mark, so you can see how far it goes before you earn the first. A feat that belongs to a land shows once someone who shares has found that land, some of them first as a riddle. Iron for the Forge now waits for its land too. The targets come from a real group's play so far and may still change. The Charcoal Burners counts the coal you take, so a kiln a Stoker's Chest feeds counts too. On a feat that adds your record to what Hearthwoven counted, each player's book can show a slightly different total.
- **Stoker's Chest feeding shows apart.** Hall > Smelters shows what a Stoker's Chest fed as its own part, never as yours; the Forgekeeper's line counts what you fed by hand.
- **Time periods on every Deeds page:** 10 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour, 3 hours, this session, Today, 7 days, 30 days and All, with the filters working inside them. A Deeds page opens on All until you pick a period. On a fellow player's book the periods show grayed, with one line that says why. The Overview stays on All, because its titles are earned over all your play. Pieces placed per kind, crops picked per kind and animals tamed are kept per day from your first 0.7 session.
- **Sort a list:** Most, A-Z or By category above the lists on Building, Crafting (by kind) and Fishing. Sorting only changes the order.
- **One bar form across the book:** rounded parts with small gaps, and under each bar a short list with name, number and share in %. Long lists sit in two columns; every part keeps its own row and its own sliver in the bar, and a tiny one says "less than 1 %", never 0. An item keeps its own color and wood keeps its grain; a category such as a hammer tab keeps one color on every page. Damage-type bars carry their numbers inside. A closed filter on Building, Crafting and Cooking still shows its first bar.
- **Fireside:** thinner lines for small counts and larger arrowheads, so the direction of a gift is easy to see. The list beside the fire says once per section where its numbers were counted, each row on one line above its arrow. A feast is set out, never made: its servings get a row of their own ("set out a Mountains feast · × 3 enjoyed"). A player is never credited with more of a dish than they made, and a craft that makes several (4 sausages, 4 Queen's Jam) counts as all of them.
- **Fellow players show sooner.** Someone who just joined shows at once as a dimmed "just joined" until their book arrives. Your first update goes 10 seconds after you spawn (was 30), then every 2 minutes (was 5). A config still on the old default of 5 moves to 2 once; if you choose 5 again, it stays.
- **Fellow players show at once after you log in.** Your PC keeps each fellow player's last shared copy for that world, so their pages show straight away, marked "as Edda last shared it", until the server's fresh copy arrives. The copies, and a small file of their running totals for Recent and While away, are plain files on your PC, per world, for up to 14 days; switching sharing off deletes them, and a fellow who leaves the server's list is deleted too.
- **This session means your session.** A fellow player's numbers in This session count only what they did while you were playing, on Recent, Battle and Together; someone who was not on says so ("Edda has not played since 21:40."). A fellow player's blocks and parries are kept for their whole session, so on Defense they show in All only, and the page says so.
- **Live updates every 10 seconds** (needs 0.7 on the server). Between full updates, each player who shares sends only what changed, a few hundred bytes. With the book open you see fellow players' numbers move while you play. The server keeps only the latest small update for each player, in memory. Your fellow players then see what you do, the land you fight in included, about every 10 seconds instead of every few minutes: the same numbers as before, sooner. A fellow player still on 0.6.5 sends every 5 minutes, so their numbers move at that pace, and Recent names them ("Edda's Hearthwoven is older: choose Session.").
- **The lands you found** now travel with your shared copy, so a group feat's land counts for everyone who shares. Still never a place.
- **Titles page** uses its full height, and "More below" no longer covers a card. A title you hold only through the game's own count says so ("Stone and ore broken before 9 October").
- **New pictures** for Recent, While away, Titles and Many Crafts, One Hall, and line pictures for the totals on While away.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-09, server build; not published)
The first build of the 0.6 branch, made to run on the server; it also carries everything under Next and Unreleased (feats) below.
- The server keeps a book of what only it can see whole, and sends each player their part with the group list (only while you share, like everything else in the group):
  - Voyages > Sailing: **Cargo loaded and unloaded**. For every ship and cart the server follows what goes in and what comes out, first in, first out. When cargo comes out somewhere else, the straight line between where it went in and where it came out, times the count, is credited as "sent" to whoever loaded it and as "delivered" to whoever unloaded it (item-km per item; two numbers, never added together). Chests do not count (storage, not transport); a change with nobody at the cart or ship credits nobody. The book starts with the chest logs the server already has (8 Oct on our own server), so the iron haul of that evening is in it.
  - Deeds > Taming: **Born near you**. Every tamed young animal the server sees appear for the first time within 40 m of you, whoever's PC hosted it. Near, not bred: the game does not record who fed them. Wild young are not counted.
  - Both sit outside the two zones with their own line saying the server counted them; "Cargo carried" (at the helm or pulling, this PC) and "Born in your care" stay beside them. Company > Together keeps Cargo carried as its cargo category.
- Faster group sharing: the server packs each fellow's copy once and sends only the copies that changed (every copy again every 5 minutes). Measured with 8 fellows and the panel open for 10 minutes: 81% less traffic, server time per request from about 2.1 ms to 0.3 ms. Nothing changes in the messages; older Hearthwoven versions keep working on both sides.
- Server files: `BepInEx/Hearthwoven/server-book-<world>.json` (saved with every world save, so a restart without a save rolls it back with the world) and `births-<date>.jsonl` (one line per birth).
